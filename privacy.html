Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

This policy covers Red Panda Poster, a private tool the owner of Red Panda Science uses to publish their own videos to the Red Panda Science YouTube channel and Instagram account. It isn't offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube Data API): permission to upload videos to the owner's channel (youtube.upload) and to read the channel's name (youtube.readonly) so it can check it's posting to the right channel.
  • Instagram (Instagram API): permission to publish Reels to the owner's account and to read the account's username.

How it's used

The tool only uploads videos the owner has made and approved, with their titles and captions. It doesn't read, collect, store or share data about viewers or anyone else, and it doesn't use Google or Meta data for advertising or analytics.

Where it's stored

The sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's and Meta's own APIs. Google user data is used in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account permissions page, or in Instagram under Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites.

Contact

Questions: send a message to @redpandascience on Instagram.
